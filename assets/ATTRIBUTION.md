# Icon sources

Microsoft Word and Excel product logos are the original SVG assets from Microsoft's Office CDN, downloaded without modification:

- https://res.cdn.office.net/files/fabric-cdn-prod_20230815.002/assets/brand-icons/product/svg/word_48x1.svg
- https://res.cdn.office.net/files/fabric-cdn-prod_20230815.002/assets/brand-icons/product/svg/excel_48x1.svg

The Microsoft product names and logos belong to Microsoft. They identify the simulated Word and Excel windows in this portfolio; the portfolio is not a Microsoft product.

The Works wordmark is a custom design for this portfolio.
